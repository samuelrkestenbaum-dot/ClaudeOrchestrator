# External-repository onboarding package

**Status: preparation. This package is not outreach.** No document here is
addressed to a prospect, and nothing here should be sent to anyone as it
stands. It exists so that the day a candidate repository does appear, the
questions, the disclosures, the forms and the exit are already written down —
and written down honestly, before anyone has an incentive to soften them.

**Evidence position, stated once and true everywhere in this package:** all
evidence for Gravito to date is *internal*. No external customer has run it, no
third party has checked it, and no controlled economic result exists. Every
number anyone has for this product was produced by the people who built it, on
their own repositories. That is not a disclaimer to be buried; it is the reason
the design-partner pilot exists at all
(`build-os/partner/DESIGN_PARTNER_PACKAGE.md`).

## The ten artifacts, and the order they are used in

| # | artifact | used when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [`ELIGIBILITY_QUESTIONNAIRE.md`](ELIGIBILITY_QUESTIONNAIRE.md) | first conversation — what we need to know about the repository |
| 2 | [`DATA_HANDLING.md`](DATA_HANDLING.md) | first conversation — what the software does with their code and secrets |
| 3 | [`INSTALL_PREREQUISITES.md`](INSTALL_PREREQUISITES.md) | before anything is installed — what must already be true |
| 4 | [`READ_ONLY_PREFLIGHT.md`](READ_ONLY_PREFLIGHT.md) | before anything is installed — how to look without writing a byte |
| 5 | [`SUPPORTED_STACKS.md`](SUPPORTED_STACKS.md) | before commitment — what the tooling can actually read |
| 6 | [`UNSUPPORTED_DISCLOSURE.md`](UNSUPPORTED_DISCLOSURE.md) | before commitment — what it cannot do, in plain words |
| 7 | [`AUTHORITY_WORKSHEET.md`](AUTHORITY_WORKSHEET.md) | before the first governed session — who may approve what |
| 8 | [`BASELINE_CAPTURE.md`](BASELINE_CAPTURE.md) | the week before the pilot — measuring the world without Gravito |
| 9 | [`SUCCESS_METRIC_TEMPLATE.md`](SUCCESS_METRIC_TEMPLATE.md) | before the pilot starts — what would count as working |
| 10 | [`UNINSTALL_ROLLBACK.md`](UNINSTALL_ROLLBACK.md) | available from day one — how to leave |

**Artifact 10 is deliberately available from day one.** A customer who cannot
see the exit before they enter is being asked for trust they have no basis to
give.

## What this package deliberately does not contain

- **No pitch, no deck, no email, no sequence.** Outreach is a separate decision
  taken by the operator, not a by-product of having written the materials.
- **No pricing.** Provisional, non-binding figures live in
  `build-os/partner/DESIGN_PARTNER_PACKAGE.md` §7 and are the operator's to
  change. Nothing here restates them, so nothing here can contradict them.
- **No scoring of candidate repositories.** The questionnaire asks questions
  and stops. A score would dress a judgment up as an assessment.
- **No claim of an outcome.** No document here says what a customer will get,
  because nobody has measured what a customer gets.
