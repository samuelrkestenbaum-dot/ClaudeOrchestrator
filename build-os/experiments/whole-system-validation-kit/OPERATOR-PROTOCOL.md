# OPERATOR PROTOCOL (operationalizes rev 3 §B + PACKET §6)
Allowed uncharged: deliver capsule; approve in-bounds authority requests;
answer from the frozen FAQ verbatim (charge 0 both arms). Charged
intervention: anything else said/done toward the task — logged with
minutes + transcript. Prohibited rescue (task-void for that arm): writing/
editing product code, dictating approach, debugging on the arm's behalf.
Emergency stop: always allowed; voids the stopped arm's task; triggers veto
review. Metering: clock from beginning-to-read the arm's request to final
response; meter events per telemetry schema.
Counterbalancing: AB/BA balanced per block of 4 pairs; washout ≥4h or
distractor between arms of one pair [OWNER may strengthen]; no cross-arm
work-product visibility (separate clones/sessions; never open X's diff
while running Y). Contamination incident: stop pair, log incident, pair is
ITT-charged or re-drawn ONLY per the frozen infeasibility/exclusion rules —
never by preference. Operator-learning disclosure: single-operator design;
order/period analysis reported; 4-pair second-operator audit per rev 3 §B;
limitation carried into every claim.

Rev 4 (red-team): the FAQ is frozen at freeze and UNAMENDABLE after the
first run; questions outside it are always charged interventions; per-arm
FAQ hit counts are logged and reported.
